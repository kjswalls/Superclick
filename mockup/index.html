    Runs that remember: meta-progression without the grind
    
              
    By Mara Okafor · Oct 28, 2026
    
              
    Most roguelites bolt a currency onto death: you lose the run, you keep some coins, you buy a permanent +5% damage. It works, but it turns every run into a chore you pay for in advance.
    
              
    The alternative is to make the world remember instead of the player. A shopkeeper you saved shows up three runs later with a better stock. A bridge you burned stays burned. Progress becomes a story the map tells back to you, and a failed run still changes something you can see on the next attempt.
    
              
    We tried both in our last prototype. Players with the currency system quit after a median of 11 runs. Players with the remembering world kept going for 26, and they described their runs to each other as stories.
